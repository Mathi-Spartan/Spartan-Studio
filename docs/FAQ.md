# FAQ

**Are these apps free?**
Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases.

**Why aren't they on the Play Store?**
They started as personal apps for my own phone. Sharing the APKs here is the simplest way to let anyone try them.

**Is it safe to install an APK?**
Install APKs only from sources you trust. Every app here is one I use daily on my own phone, and each release lists a SHA-256 checksum so you can confirm the file you downloaded is the one I published. See [how to check it](INSTALL.md#check-your-download-optional).

**Do the apps collect my data?**
No. There are no accounts and no servers. Your notes, health data and reading history stay on your phone. (Suvadi only goes online to fetch a book’s text from Project Madurai the first time you open it.) Each app's page lists every permission it asks for and why.

**What does "built with AI" mean?**
I design each app (the idea, the look and every decision) and build it with an AI pair programmer, [Claude](https://claude.ai) by Anthropic, which helps write and review the code. I approve every design and test every build on my own phone before it's published.

**Which phones are supported?**
Suvadi needs Android 8+, Uyir needs Android 9+, and Kurippu needs Android 11+. They're tested on a CMF Phone Pro 2.

**I found a bug or have an idea.**
Please [open an issue](https://github.com/Mathi-Spartan/Spartan-Studio/issues) and tell me your phone model and Android version.
