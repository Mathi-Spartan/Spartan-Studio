# Kurippu (குறிப்பு) — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 3 November 2026*

Kurippu is a notes app for Android, made by Mathi (developer account on Google Play: Mathi-Spartan). This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version
- **Your notes stay on your phone.** Kurippu has no servers and no accounts.
- Kurippu does **not** collect, sell or share your notes or any personal information.
- There are **no ads** and **no analytics or tracking** in Kurippu.

## What Kurippu stores, and where
Your notes, checklists, tags and reminders are saved in a private database on your phone. Only Kurippu can read it.

- **Android backup:** if you have turned on Google backup for your phone, Android includes Kurippu's notes in that backup so they return when you reinstall or change phones. This is handled by Android and your Google account, under Google's terms, and you can switch it off in your phone's settings.
- **Backup files:** when you tap *Back up now*, Kurippu writes one file containing your notes to the place you choose (for example your phone storage or Google Drive). Kurippu only creates that file when you ask, and never uploads it anywhere itself.

## Features that use other parts of your phone
- **Voice notes** use your phone's own speech-to-text service (usually Google). Kurippu receives only the text it returns. That service is provided under its maker's own privacy terms.
- **Scan a page** uses your camera app to take a photo, then reads the text **on your phone** with Google ML Kit text recognition. The photo is kept only briefly in Kurippu's private cache and is deleted on the next scan. The text-recognition component is supplied through Google Play services, which may download the text model and may send Google basic diagnostic information about how the component is performing. It does not send your photos or your notes.
- **Reminders** use Android's alarms and notifications. They work entirely on your phone.

## Permissions
| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | To show the reminders you set |
| Alarms and reminders (exact alarms) | So a reminder rings at exactly the time you chose |
| Run at startup | To set your reminders again after the phone restarts |
| Internet / network state | Required by Google's on-device text-recognition component for setup. Kurippu never uploads your notes |

## Children
Kurippu is a general notes app and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

## Deleting your data
Delete any note inside the app, or uninstall Kurippu to remove all its data from your phone. Backup files you saved yourself stay wherever you put them until you delete them.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
